How to update this website

Edit the DATA section in the JavaScript. Add a month to monthlyPartyPolls and/or monthlyBlocs. Add parties to parties, including their colours and political blocs. Update latestComparison and electionResults when you have new figures. Use null for missing figures.

Polling figures are based on the supplied Tushtoan data. Chart.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required.
